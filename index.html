Aapka data sirf aapke phone me hai. Hum (app banane wale) kuch nahi dekhte.

Google Drive me backup

Sirf agar aap chaho. Kaagzaat seedha aapke phone se aapke apne Google Drive me jaate hain. Hum tak ya kisi server tak kuch nahi aata.

Backup is password se encrypt hota hai, to Drive me bhi koi usse padh nahi sakta. Password bhoolne par backup wapas nahi aayega.

App Lock (PIN)

Ye app kholne par rok lagata hai (phone chhupne ke 1 minute baad dobara band). PIN bhool gaye to wapas nahi milega, bas app ka data saaf karna padega. Isliye Drive backup bhi rakho.

Emergency card

Blood group aur ghar waalon ke number. Lock screen se bhi ek tap me dikhta hai, isliye sirf wahi bharo jo sabko dikhana theek ho.

Expiry ki yaad

Har kaagaz par "Calendar me yaad lagao" dabao. Phone ka calendar 30, 7 aur 1 din pehle yaad dilayega, app band ho tab bhi. Neeche wala notification sirf app kholne par dikhta hai.

Aapki privacy

Kaagzaat sirf aapke phone me rehte hain. Drive backup chalu karo to wo aapke apne Google Drive me, aapke password se encrypt ho kar jaate hain. App banane waale ko kuch nahi dikhta. Aadhaar bhejte waqt masked copy (sirf aakhri 4 ank) behtar hai, wo UIDAI ki site se mil jaati hai. Ye app sarkari nahi hai.

Gulf mein madad ke raaste

  • MADAD Bharat sarkar ka consular shikayat aur madad portal.
  • e-Migrate Kaam ke liye jaane se pehle agent aur offer ki jaanch.
  • Absher Saudi mein iqama aur visa ki asli tareekh yahin dekho.

Ye list sirf madad ke liye hai, sarkari app nahi. Yojna ki sahi aur taaza jaankari hamesha sarkari site par dekho.

Aap kin yojnaon ke liye ho sakte ho?

Apni jaankari bharo. Yeh sirf andaza hai, jaankari aapke phone se bahar nahi jaati.

Rules badalte rehte hain, isliye aakhri faisla hamesha sarkari portal par dekho. Yojna mein shamil karne ke naam par bada paisa maangne wale agent se savdhan raho.
